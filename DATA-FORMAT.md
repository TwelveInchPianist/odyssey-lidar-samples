# Reading the data

- `narrow-original.hevc`, `wide-original.hevc`: unchanged full native segment. Use a normal HEVC decoder. Camera ordinals in metadata are zero-based decoded-frame numbers. Native timestamp/index records accompany each camera.
- Preview MP4: selected original ordinals at20 fps, resized to960 x602, reencoded H.264. No brightening or enhancement. Use original HEVC for analysis.
- `lidar-selected-scans.pcap`: selected complete scans; **not a continuous capture**. Raw PCAP record bytes and UDP payloads are copied unchanged from the application-level recording. Original recording used DLT_RAW IPv4 wrappers, not physical Ethernet capture. Camera segmentation and PCAP filenames do not share a numbering scheme.
- Raw M1 points can be decoded with RoboSense rs_driver for **RSM1**, not M1 Plus/M2. The M1 user guide is https://cdn.robosense.cn/20211220200638_20760.pdf and official driver is https://github.com/RoboSense-LiDAR/rs_driver . A PCAP gap here often reflects intentional excerpt selection; consult the scan-key list.
- Case01/02 decoded `.npz`: `sensor_xyz` (float32 metres), `intensity` (sensor byte stored numerically), `point_device_ns` (int64 internal device timestamps). This convenience export keeps valid decoded returns with the SDK-style0.2–200 m range and valid-angle mask. It applies **no ground or object filter**. The raw PCAP preserves the original payload including entries excluded by that decode mask.
- Sensor XYZ: X forward, Y left, Z up. Driver-perspective angular plots show rightward angle as negative atan2(Y,X). Logged body/vehicle positions use the pipeline's configured transform; distance forward from that origin is not slant range from the lidar optical origin.
- `recording-configuration.json` contains the historical estimated transform. It is not independently surveyed or optically validated. `alignment-and-frame-context.json` records the actual camera index, chosen lidar context, clock conversion and uncertainty.
- Camera native timestamps, laptop monotonic timestamps, lidar device timestamps and PCAP wall timestamps are **different domains**. Use recorded mappings. Small clock-fit residuals do not establish physical exposure-to-laser simultaneity. Do not align sensors merely by file number or wall-clock timestamp.
- `SHA256-MANIFEST.json` verifies files inside each ZIP. `pcap-extraction.json` identifies selected complete scans and packet counts. The release-level checksum file covers all downloadable assets.
- Case04 contains all saved offline replay rows and virtual proposals, plus representative selected raw scans. It is not a standalone full-minute replay dataset: complete raw scans, full telemetry history and engine dependencies would be needed to rerun the entire minute. Selected raw excerpts are sufficient to inspect their geometry without filtering.
