        Parked path-alignment calibration

        Use an empty, level paved area with the vehicle parked and ignition on. Apply the ground proposal first, then start a new parked collection. Use one upright cardboard box at least 0.6 m wide and 0.8 m tall. Measure positions from the point on the ground directly beneath the comma road-facing camera; positive sideways distance is left.

        1. Remove the box and capture the empty scene.
        2. Place the box at each of three measured, separated positions: for example 10 m forward / 0 m left; 10 m forward / 2 m left; 20 m forward / 0 m left. Wait at least two seconds at each position, enter its coordinates, and capture it.
        3. Move it to a fourth position, such as 15 m forward / 1.5 m right (enter −1.5). Use the independent-check button.
        4. Stop collection, then apply alignment if the independent check passed. Timing and road-performance validation remain separate.

        Timing calibration review

        After parked alignment, record two separate ordinary driving sessions in silent mode. Each needs clear static surroundings and at least 15 seconds of natural turning variation. Do not stage hazardous encounters or make unusual maneuvers. Stop after each session and let automatic replay finish.

        The review compares independently estimated lidar motion with comma motion. It offers a timing adjustment only when two compatible sessions agree with narrow estimates. This is an empirical alignment check, not a hardware timing guarantee or proof of warning performance.
